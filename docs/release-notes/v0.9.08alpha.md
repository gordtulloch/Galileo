## Galileo v0.9.08alpha

Alpha pre-release. Please try it and report problems, but **do not use it for production imaging yet.**

Changes since v0.9.07alpha.

- **Phone and tablet app (MobileConnect):** run your saved sessions from the phone and manage the job queue (pause, stop, reorder, remove); autofocus now shows its V-curve and HFR; guiding gets Connect and Loop buttons; starting polar alignment, solve results and filter names from the app are fixed. The Android app is on the download page (needs MobileConnect plug-in 0.1.2).
- **Sessions:** Save asks for a name, and saved and scheduled sessions are kept until they complete or you delete them. Scheduled sessions now start even if you never opened the Sessions screen.
- **Star Atlas:** the IC catalog is added, every saved target is always drawn, Find plots everything a name could mean, and the object you Select is plotted at once instead of showing empty sky. New right-click menu: Save to Targets, Remove from Targets, Park and Unpark.
- **Planning:** new Targets list (the old catalog screen is now Search) and an Astrospheric hourly forecast on What's Up Tonight.
- **Solve and Imaging:** extra ASTAP command-line options (Options > Solve), a "Subtract dark" preview option, and ASTAP solves that worked standalone now work from Galileo. The Solve "Dark" checkbox is gone.
- **Fixes:** autofocus V-curve no longer scrambled by defocused stars, Connect All no longer reports INDI devices missing, no more "Python quit unexpectedly" popups, and survey thumbnails come from a working mirror.
