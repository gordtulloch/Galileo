## Galileo v0.9.10alpha

Alpha pre-release. Please try it and report problems, but **do not use it for production imaging yet.**

Changes since v0.9.09alpha.

### Read this before upgrading: weather now moves your equipment

- **Unsafe weather now triggers a protective stop.** Galileo stops sessions and other activity (including the exposure in progress) and guiding, parks every mount in the Observatory, and closes the dome or roof once they confirm they are parked. Nothing starts again until conditions have been safe (15 minutes by default) and you acknowledge.
- **Both the automatic park and the roof close are on after upgrading.** Check your rules on Equipment > Safety first. A rule can be set to **Warn** instead of **Stop**, and the page has switches for the park and the roof close.
- **A saved safety device that is not connected now counts as unsafe**, and weather-station rules count as much as a Safety Monitor.
- **Automatic roof closes park the telescopes first.** If any mount cannot be confirmed parked, including one that is configured but switched off, the roof is left open and you are alerted.
- This has been tested with simulated devices only. **Rehearse it in daylight, and keep hardware rain protection**: nothing here works if the computer or network fails. Downgrading is not tested; back up your database first.

### Also new

- **Library > Calibration:** run auto-calibration from inside Galileo and watch its output.
- **Dark frames:** the Imaging and Solve screens say whether a matching dark master is available, and the solver uses it. Load & Slew can take a frame from the Library.
- **Sessions:** each shows an estimated total time, used as its scheduled duration. Two identical blocks are no longer mistaken for each other.
- **Imaging:** Object field, camera cooling controls, and the nudge pad moved to a Mount Control dialog with an emergency stop.
- **Guiding:** calibration progress is shown, and calibration is no longer reported as a lost star.
- **Cameras:** the Offset is now applied on Alpaca cameras and on INDI cameras such as ZWO.
- **Star Atlas:** the Milky Way background now appears.
- **Plug-ins:** settings open from the Plugins screen, plug-ins have icons, and newly installed solvers appear without a restart. The Skyshot download certificate error is fixed.

The full list is in [docs/changelog/0.9.10alpha.md](https://github.com/gordtulloch/Galileo/blob/main/docs/changelog/0.9.10alpha.md).
