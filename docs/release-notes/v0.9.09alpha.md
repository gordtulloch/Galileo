## Galileo v0.9.09alpha

Alpha pre-release. Please try it and report problems, but **do not use it for production imaging yet.**

Changes since v0.9.08alpha.

- **Event notices:** every significant event (exposure start, slew start and end, plate-solve result, focus result, equipment connection) now appears as a small panel over the top-right corner of the window and closes itself after 10 seconds. They replace the log panes on every screen. The panels also reach the phone and tablet apps (needs MobileConnect plug-in 0.1.3 and Android app 0.9.9).
- **Plate solving:** plug-ins can add solvers. New Skyshot Solver plug-in (blind solving, with its star index downloaded from Options).
- **Phone connection:** a phone can now connect when a VPN or virtual network adapter made Galileo pick the wrong network address.
- **Sessions:** moving blocks no longer crashes or freezes the window on macOS.
- **Plug-ins:** all first-party plug-ins are rebuilt with this release; MobileConnect is now 0.1.3.
