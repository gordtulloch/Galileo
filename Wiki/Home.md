# Galileo Wiki

![Galileo logo](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/gordtulloch/Galileo/main/assets/images/logo.png)

**Galileo** is a cross-platform astrophotography imaging application for Windows, macOS, Linux and Raspberry Pi, built on [INDI](https://indilib.org/) and [ASCOM Alpaca](https://ascom-standards.org/AlpacaDeveloper/). It supports a mix of INDI and Alpaca devices, multiple piers per observatory, and multiple optical tubes and cameras per mount.

> Galileo is **pre-alpha**. It runs today, but some screens are still placeholders. The [README](https://github.com/gordtulloch/Galileo#status) lists what is built.
