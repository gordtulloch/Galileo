# Switches

The Switches page lists the switch devices (relays, power ports and similar) found at a server. For the full set of controls of a particular device, use the Aux page.

Choose the **Driver**, enter the **Server** and **Port**, then press **Connect** to list the devices available. Press **Save** to remember the settings for this Pier.

## Connect

On this page, queries the server and refreshes the list of switch devices it offers.
