# Flat Panel

The Flat Panel page controls an illuminated panel used to take flat frames: either a motorised dust cover with a built-in light (such as a Flip-Flat), or a light panel fixed in the observatory. The Flats Assistant on the Imaging screen and flat blocks in sessions use it automatically.

Set-up: choose the **Driver**, **Server**, press **Scan**, pick the panel, **Connect**, then **Save**.

## Panel Type

**Flat Panel**: a motorised cover plus a light (the Cover and Light controls both apply). **Observatory Panel**: a fixed, light-only panel with no cover, so the cover controls are hidden.

## Cover

Whether the dust cover is closed, open, moving or unknown, as reported by the device. Also the heading of the cover controls below.

## Light

Whether the panel's light is currently on or off.

## Park

Closes the dust cover over the telescope. Do this when you finish for the night, to keep the optics clean.

## Unpark

Opens the dust cover so the telescope can see the sky. When taking flats with the panel, the cover stays closed over the telescope and you use the light instead.

## Flat Light

The panel's light: use **Light On** and **Light Off**.

## Light On

Turns the panel's light on at the brightness set below.

## Light Off

Turns the panel's light off.

## Brightness

How bright the panel shines, from 0 to the device's maximum. Drag the slider or type a value, then press **Set**. For flats, the Flats Assistant chooses the brightness that gives the right exposure for you.

## Set

Applies the brightness chosen on the slider or in the box.
