# Equipment

Equipment is where you tell Galileo what hardware you have: one page for each kind of device. Settings are stored per **Pier** (one mount with its telescope(s) and devices) within an **Observatory**, using the Observatory and Pier selectors in the top bar. Work through the categories in the left menu, setting each up the same way:

1. Choose the **Driver**: Alpaca or INDI.
2. Enter the **Server** (and **Port**) of the machine the driver runs on, and press **Scan** to list the devices it offers.
3. Pick the device in **Device** and press **Connect**.
4. Press **Save** so Galileo remembers it for this Pier.

After that, the **Connect** button in the top bar connects every saved device at once, and "Connect on startup" does so when Galileo starts.
