# Optics

The Optics page describes the telescope tubes (or camera lenses) on this Pier. Galileo uses the focal length to work out your image scale and field of view for framing, plate solving and the Star Atlas, and the image alignment to draw frames the right way round.

Each **Optical Tube** panel is one telescope. A Pier can have several, for example a main scope and a guide scope, or two tubes on one mount. Choose which tube is in use with the Optics selector in the top bar. Press **Save** when you are done.

## Name

A name for this tube, such as "Esprit 100ED". It appears in the Optics selector at the top of the window.

## Focal Length

The tube's focal length in millimetres. With a reducer or flattener, enter the *effective* focal length with it fitted. It sets the image scale (arcseconds per pixel) together with the camera's pixel size, so an inaccurate value makes plate solves and framing less accurate.

## Aperture

The diameter of the main lens or mirror, in millimetres. Used to show the focal ratio and for image-scale calculations.

## Optical System

The kind of telescope: Newtonian, Schmidt-Cassegrain, Mak-Cassegrain, Refractor or Other.

## Image Alignment

How the image is oriented compared with the sky, depending on the optics between the sky and the sensor. Tick **Reversed** and/or **Inverted** to match what your frames show.

## Reversed

The image is mirrored left-to-right, for example with a refractor and a star diagonal.

## Inverted

The image is flipped top-to-bottom, as it is with a Newtonian.

## Associated

The devices that belong to this tube — for instance the camera, filter wheel and focuser at its focus. Press **+** to add one of the devices already saved on this Pier. Press **Remove** beside one to dissociate it.

## Associate device

Associates a device already configured on this Pier (saved on one of the other Equipment pages) with this tube. If every saved device is already associated, Galileo tells you to save one first.

## Add optical tube

Adds another optical tube panel. The first is *Optical Tube 1*; the rest can be removed.
