# Planning

Planning helps you decide what to image and when. It has four screens, chosen from the menu down the left:

- **What's Up Tonight** — a ranked list of the best targets for tonight from your observatory.
- **Targets** — search the catalogs for objects by name, type, brightness, size or visibility.
- **Sessions** — build a capture plan from blocks (slew, focus, image…) and run or schedule it.
- **Schedule** — a calendar of what is planned over the coming nights.

The Observatory's latitude and longitude (set in the top bar) are needed for anything that depends on the sky at your site.
